About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Arizona State University, working with Prof. Krishnendu Chakrabarty (ACM/IEEE/AAAS Fellow) on low-energy AI computing for space applications and radiation-hard AI chips, in collaboration with Sandia National Laboratories.

I received my Doctor of Engineering (Dr.-Ing.) degree with Summa Cum Laude from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), advised by Prof. Mehdi B. Tahoori (IEEE Fellow). My doctoral thesis, "Scalable and Efficient Methods for Uncertainty Estimation and Reduction in Edge AI Accelerators," addressed the intersection of hardware reliability, Bayesian inference, and in-memory computing.

My research spans reliable AI accelerators, in-memory computing (RRAM, Spintronics, MRAM), uncertainty estimation, fault tolerance in ML systems, hardware-aware LLM optimization, and HW/SW co-design. I am also the Founder of MemoryCentric.AI, focused on efficient LLM optimization.
